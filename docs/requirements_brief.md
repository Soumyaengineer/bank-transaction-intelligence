# Requirements Brief — Bank Customer & Transaction Intelligence

## 1. Stakeholder & context
Priya Menon is the Head of Cards at HarborTrust Bank, a mid-size retail bank with a growing credit card portfolio. She is under pressure to lift card usage, retention and upsell performance while fraud losses are rising and customer complaints about payment friction are increasing. The bank needs a clearer view of where card value is being created and where fraud controls are creating the biggest business trade-offs.

## 2. The decision they need to make
This brief supports a decision on where HarborTrust should focus customer growth and retention spend across cardholder segments, while also identifying where fraud controls need to be tightened. The goal is not simply to describe transactions, but to decide where the bank can protect revenue without creating unnecessary friction for good, high-value customers.

## 3. Three business questions
**Q1 — Customer value:** Which customer segments, based on age band, income band and credit-score band, generate the highest transaction value, transaction volume and customer activity?

**Q2 — Fraud exposure:** Where is labelled fraud concentrated across merchant category, card brand or type, and transaction channel such as chip, online or swipe?

**Q3 — Value versus risk overlap:** Which high-value customer segments also show higher fraud exposure, and where would stricter controls risk disrupting the bank’s most valuable customers?

## 4. Agreed KPIs
**Total transaction value:** The total dollar value of transactions across the selected customer, card and transaction segments.

**Average transaction value:** Total transaction value divided by the number of transactions, showing the typical transaction size.

**Transactions per customer:** Total transactions divided by the number of unique customers, showing customer activity and card engagement.

**Fraud rate %:** The percentage of transactions labelled as fraudulent, calculated only on transactions with a fraud label.

**Fraud $ exposure:** The total dollar value of transactions labelled as fraudulent, showing the direct value at risk.

**% value from top decile of customers:** The share of total transaction value generated by the highest-spending 10% of customers.

## 5. Out of scope
- Fraud prediction or machine learning models.
- Real-time transaction monitoring or alerting.
- Merchant-level fraud investigation.
- Geographic analysis beyond state level.
- The 33% unlabelled transactions when calculating fraud metrics.
